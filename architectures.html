Supplement to the architecture-tuning appendix

Every architecture in the search, animated

The architecture search compared 37 configurations of a convolutional autoencoder across three stages. Each diagram below animates one of them, following a batch of six environmental variables over 6,935 days as the encoder compresses the record to five numbers and the decoder rebuilds it. Tensor shapes and receptive fields are computed from the convolution arithmetic rather than drawn by hand, so the picture agrees with the thesis tables.

How to read the diagrams

Every diagram is a hairpin. The encoder runs left to right along the top, the latent vector sits at the right-hand turn, and the decoder runs right to left along the bottom, so the reconstruction finishes directly underneath the input it is trying to reproduce.

strided convolution, halves the time axis dilated convolution, keeps the length, widens the view pooling or interpolation, no learned weights fully connected bridge latent vector

The bar sliding above an active layer is the kernel. Its seven tick marks are the seven weights. When the dilation rate rises the marks fan apart while their number stays the same, which is the entire mechanism behind the wider receptive field. Each diagram plays when it scrolls into view and can be paused or scrubbed.

The three operators

The full diagrams run at 6,935 timesteps, where individual kernel positions cannot be resolved. These three explainers show the same operators at a readable scale.

Strided convolution

A strided layer moves the kernel two positions at a time, so the output holds one position for every two inputs and the sequence approximately halves. The window is seven wide and the step is two, so consecutive windows overlap by five positions. Every input value is still read, which is why halving the length does not discard half the record.

Dilated convolution

A dilated layer keeps the stride at one, so the sequence length is unchanged, and instead spaces the seven weights apart by the dilation rate. At a rate of four the kernel spans 25 positions while still holding seven weights, and the 18 positions in between are simply not consulted at that layer. Stacking rates of one, two, four and eight is what carries the final model to a receptive field of 763 days without adding parameters.

Adaptive pooling

Dilated stacks arrive at the bridge still holding their full length, so a separate reduction is needed. Adaptive average pooling divides the axis into 109 bins and averages within each. The ratio is not a whole number, so neighbouring bins overlap by one position. Global average pooling is the same idea taken to one bin, which is why the Dilated A configuration lost the temporal detail and failed to reconstruct.

The selected model

Three strided layers compress the record, then four dilated layers widen the view across more than two annual cycles before the bridge. This is the model carried into the clustering and regionalisation analyses.

Stage 1, encoder topology

Stage 1 asked whether the six variables should share one convolutional stack or receive one each. Sharing won despite reconstructing less accurately, because the separate stacks multiplied the bridge input sixfold and produced a latent space that failed the effective-rank check.

Stage 2, convolutional operator

Stage 2 held the topology fixed and varied how the temporal axis is processed and reduced. The eight configurations differ mainly in where the compression happens, which is visible in the diagrams as the point at which the slabs start to shorten.

Stage 3, refinement sweeps

Stage 3 varied one design choice at a time over the retained skeleton. Because the variants share a shape, they share a diagram: choose a sweep, then a configuration within it.

Notes on the diagrams

Stride two does not skip inputs. With a seven-wide kernel and a step of two, consecutive windows overlap by five positions, so every day in the record is read. What halves is the number of output positions, not the amount of data consulted.

The receptive field is not the memory of the model. A figure such as 763 days is the span of input feeding one convolutional position before the flatten. After the pooling and the fully connected bridge, every latent dimension depends on all 6,935 days.

The decoder does not invert the encoder. The transposed convolutions learn their own weights rather than reusing the encoder weights, and the recovery from the pooled length back to the convolutional length is linear interpolation carrying no parameters at all.

Reconstructions here are illustrative. The traces in the input and output panels are generated to show the shape of the task, a seasonal cycle with intermittent rainfall, rather than plotted from the dataset. The measured reconstruction quality is the fraction of variance unexplained reported on the overview page and in the thesis.
