Feature distributions by region

This figure shows the complete distributions of the 33 climate features under the eight Köppen-Geiger classes and the eight feature-learning regions. The thesis reports the feature-level variance comparison and principal findings; this page preserves the complete distributional evidence.

Reading the plots

The figure compares the 33 features across the two regionalisations. The Köppen-Geiger classes appear on the left and the feature-learning regions on the right. The panels share a vertical axis, allowing distributions at the same height to be compared directly. Wider parts of a violin contain more sites, and the horizontal line marks the median.

Select the figure to open it at full resolution. The BSk class contains only five sites, so its distribution should be interpreted cautiously.
