Complete feature-level comparison

Feature distributions by region

These plots show the complete distributions of the 33 climate features under the eight Köppen-Geiger classes and the eight feature-learning regions. The thesis reports the feature-level variance comparison and principal findings; this page preserves the complete distributional evidence.

Reading the plots

Each figure compares one feature across the two regionalisations. The Köppen-Geiger classes appear on the left and the feature-learning regions on the right. The panels share a vertical axis, allowing distributions at the same height to be compared directly. Wider parts of a violin contain more sites, and the horizontal line marks the median.

Select any plot to open the original image. The BSk class contains only five sites, so its distribution should be interpreted cautiously.

Temperature features

Eleven bioclimatic temperature features describe annual conditions, seasonal variation and temperatures during wet, dry, warm and cold periods.

Precipitation features

Eight bioclimatic precipitation features describe annual amount, seasonality and precipitation during wet, dry, warm and cold periods.

Humidity features

Eight humidity features describe mean conditions, humidity range, vapour-pressure deficit, seasonality and humidity during selected quarters.

Wind features

Six wind features describe mean wind speed, the windiest quarter, seasonality and wind speed during wet, dry and warm periods.
